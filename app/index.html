Roadmap to the platinum

Nine phases, in the order the game lets you do them. The story comes first because it unlocks everything else; the 100% checklist comes last because it asks for more than any single trophy does. One playthrough is enough — if you keep the save rules on the Missables tab.

Missables & save rules

One confirmed missable element in the whole list: the Chapter 1 balcony meditation that counts toward Positive Mental Attitude. Everything else — every side-strand finale, collectible and crime trophy — stays available after the credits.

  • No mission replay, no New Game Plus. A finished story mission cannot be selected again, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one untouched save from just before the final mission. The game gives you 15 manual slots plus an autosave.
  • Do the balcony meditation in Chapter 1. After Lucia is home, check the kitchen in the morning (a morning after the mission Down to Earth is the usual time): she heads to the balcony of Jason's house to meditate. Follow her and accept the optional task. It is gone once Chapter 1 ends. Whether the trophy strictly needs that one spot is unconfirmed — do it anyway, and keep the Chapter 1 save until the trophy pops.
  • Race Against Yago is time-sensitive. This Chapter 3 race in Yago's street racing strand (hourglass icon) counts toward Strangers & Freaks. Save first, then race it the moment it appears.
  • Never use cheats on your trophy save. Cheats switch off trophies and saving.
