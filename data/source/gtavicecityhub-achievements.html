GTA 6 Achievements

The real GTA VI trophy list: all 55, with what each one asks. The story ones stay hidden until you open them.

Reported, November 18, 2026. Push Square reports the PS5 list from Exophase: 55 trophies, including a Platinum. GTA BOOM lists all 55 by name, the same 55 on Xbox Series X|S as achievements worth 1,000 Gamerscore in total, and says about half are hidden. Sony and Microsoft publish the lists; the requirements below are in our own words. We will add each trophy’s grade when a source we trust lists it.

Before you start

Three things that save you a replay

  1. Save before big missions. Story missions cannot be replayed, IGN reports. You get 15 manual save slots.
  2. Play the story first. The chapter trophies come on their own; many activities open up as you go.
  3. Clean up after. Collectibles, stunts, races and the 100% list are easier with more of the map open.
