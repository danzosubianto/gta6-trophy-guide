The short answer

How many
55 trophies on PS5: 1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver and 46 Bronze. Xbox Series X|S has the same 55 achievements worth 1,000 Gamerscore. 28 of them are hidden.
The Platinum
Vice Got a Price! for earning every other trophy. On Xbox it should be worth 0G, since the other 54 achievements add up to the full 1,000. The two Golds are A Long Road (100% completion, 100G) and You Gotta Know When to Get Off (the final mission, 50G).
Missable
Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1 counts toward Positive Mental Attitude and disappears after that chapter. There is no mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission.
How long
Around 200 hours for the Platinum. No online trophies, no difficulty trophies and no mission medals: it is long, not hard. Cheats should switch off trophies and saving.

Save before every chapter: GTA 6 has no mission replay

You can finish the whole list in one playthrough. These four rules keep you from needing a second one.

  • No mission replay, no New Game Plus. Once a story mission is done you cannot select it again. The game has 15 manual save slots plus one autosave: keep one at the start of each chapter and an untouched one from just before the final mission, and rotate the rest.
  • Do Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1. After Lucia is home, check the kitchen in the morning (a morning after the mission Down to Earth is the usual time). She heads out to the balcony of Jason’s house to meditate. Follow her and accept the optional task. It is gone once Chapter 1 ends. It is not confirmed that Positive Mental Attitude strictly needs it, so do it anyway and keep a Chapter 1 save until the trophy pops.
  • Do Race Against Yago the moment it appears. This Chapter 3 race in Yago’s street racing strand is time-sensitive (hourglass icon) and the strand counts toward Strangers & Freaks. Save first, then race.
  • Never use cheats on your trophy save. Cheats should switch off both trophies and saving.

Everything outside the story stays available after the credits, including the side-story finales and the 100% checklist. That checklist only appears after the story, under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion, but work you do earlier still counts. Our GTA 6 walkthrough lists the main missions in order if you want to plan your chapter saves.

Robbery trophies unlock in Chapters 3 and 4

Five trophies come from robbing places, and the tools for them arrive through optional and story missions in the middle of the game.

Sneakthief: use the Snap Gun on the back door

The Chapter 3 mission Yasmani’s Jewelry Jobs gives you the Snap Gun and opens jewelry store robberies. Go to a store’s locked back door and use the Snap Gun on it. Kicking the door in sets off the alarm. Inside the back room, find the note with the safe code, open the safe, and leave without being seen. Richardson Jewelry & Diamonds in Vice City is a good target. If someone spots you, reload rather than push on.

Retail Therapy: never let the Wanted Level drop

Call your partner, then rob three shops in a row while the police are still chasing you. If the stars fade and you are only in the red heat zone, the chain breaks. The trophy can take about 30 seconds to pop after you leave the third shop, so give it a moment before you hit a fourth.

Maximum Withdrawal, Hot Together and A Classic Blitz Play

Bank vaults unlock through That’s Where The Money Is, an optional mission near the end of Chapter 4. Tail an armored security van to a large bank and get into the vault while the guard has it open. You cannot carry all the bags alone, so bring your partner. Vault jobs give you a 4-star Wanted Level straight away, so escaping one with your partner also earns Hot Together. That trophy, and A Classic Blitz Play, only pop once you have left the red heat zone, not just the flashing search area. The 100% checklist needs all eight vaults.

Pest Control and Dime on the Dollar

Pest Control needs one illegal operation robbed from each of the nine gangs. The 100% checklist wants all 39 operations, so it pops along the way. Marked bills for Dime on the Dollar come from bank and payday loan jobs and are laundered by selling them to your contacts. The $250,000 total is cumulative.

Partner trophies need Jason and Lucia together

Switching between Jason and Lucia should open up during Chapter 2, and then you can switch and call the other one in as your partner. Hot Together and Retail Therapy above need that partner with you, and so do these.

  • Positive Mental Attitude. Meditation spots show as purple icons when you are close. Each outdoor spot should count twice if you meditate there with both characters, so bring your partner every time. The Chapter 1 balcony meditation is the one you can miss.
  • Personal Best (hidden). It should pop when Jason and Lucia both reach maximum Strength. Buy a gym membership and train with your partner there, since the partner trains alongside you.

Free-roam trophies: the airliner, the tank, Combat Focus and money

Catch Me If You Can: take a jet from a boarding bridge

At Vice City International Airport (VCIA), steal a large passenger plane that is docked at a boarding bridge. Planes parked elsewhere on the airfield do not count. The planes at gates E3 and E4 work. Go up to the cockpit, back out toward the runway and take off. The trophy is delayed: fly about 1 km away from the airport and it pops. Save first, because the police come fast.

Challenging Tank

Its description, “Kinda hot in these Rhinos”, means getting into a military tank. Tanks should be parked on the south side of Mile Zero Air Station, on the island at the far southwest of the map. Save first, get in, and drive and fire until it pops.

Gunslinger

Combat Focus is the slow-motion aim: press L3 and R3 while aiming. Pick a group of gang enemies, such as the guards at an illegal operation, and use an assault rifle. Adrenaline extends Focus.

Little Boxes, On the Spot and Treat Yo Self

  • Little Boxes: buy the eight garages in the Vice City Real Estate app, which unlocks after the Chapter 2 mission Daily Driver. Together they cost about $160,000. Garages the story gives you do not count.
  • On the Spot: the Chapter 2 mission Moses opens the Scooter Bros app. Take a stolen car to Moses, choose Respray, then Register. From Chapter 3 you can also register cars with a standard tracker at Pay ‘n’ Spray. Each car needs a free garage space, so buy garages first and register cheap cars.
  • Treat Yo Self: the $100,000 is cumulative across every kind of shop.

The long grind: stunts, Challenges, vehicles and 100%

  • Cunning Stunts: stunt jumps, under-the-bridge flights, knife flights and base jumps all count. 100% needs 300, so keep going past 250.
  • One Last Thing: Challenges are listed under Progress > Challenges. 100% needs 70 of the 80, and many complete on their own while you play.
  • Whip It Good: scanning a vehicle with the WAiNK app counts as discovering it, so scan traffic as you drive. 100% needs 200 of the 277 base models.
  • Actions Speak Louder (hidden): 500 actions that change your Criminal Profile. Robberies and fights feed it, so it fills up without a separate grind.
  • Strangers & Freaks: only some optional questlines count. Andy’s, Billy’s and Tyrone’s do not, because they have their own trophies. Check the trophy’s progress counter after each strand you finish.
  • The Shootist and Top Fun: there should be two shooting ranges with 42 gold medals between them, and 14 Top Fun drone race locations. Win at least one race at each location.
  • Fair Game (hidden): Bret Vogel’s hunting questline is Jason-only and sends you after animals through target decks. A clean head or heart shot keeps the carcass pristine.

Let’s Go Bowling: rob Kingpin Bowling

The Kolt & Associates bowling alley is Kingpin Bowling, an illegal operation you visit in Chapter 2. Bring Sticky Bombs. Take all three key valuables: the luxury watch in an office, the shotgun on a table, and the contents of the office safe, which you blow open with a Sticky Bomb. Then escape the police. If you leave something behind, the operation comes back later, so it is not missable.

Side-story finales stay open after the story

Kenton, Billy, Andy, Randy, Tyrone and Bret each have a questline that ends in a trophy, and they should all stay available after the credits. Andy’s questline is Lucia-only. Tyrone’s and Bret’s are Jason-only.

Five hidden trophies with no confirmed trigger yet

In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There and Ten Pin have no confirmed unlock method at launch. Their descriptions above are the only hint, and some may pop while you play normally. We will add each method once it is confirmed.

Mistakes that cost GTA 6 trophy hunters hours

  • Mistake: Skipping Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1. Fix: Accept it when she walks out to the balcony, and keep a Chapter 1 save until Positive Mental Attitude pops.
  • Mistake: Overwriting your only save before the final mission. Fix: Keep one untouched manual save from just before it, since there is no mission replay.
  • Mistake: Testing cheats on your main save. Fix: Cheats should switch off trophies and saving. Use a throwaway save if you try them.
  • Mistake: Kicking in a jewelry store’s back door. Fix: Use the Snap Gun, or the alarm ruins Sneakthief.
  • Mistake: Stealing a plane parked out on the airfield. Fix: Catch Me If You Can needs a jet at a boarding bridge, then a flight about 1 km out.
  • Mistake: Stopping at 250 stunts. Fix: A Long Road needs 300.

FAQ

How many trophies are in GTA 6?

55: 1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver and 46 Bronze. All of them are single-player. There are no GTA Online trophies at launch.

How much Gamerscore is GTA 6 on Xbox?

1,000G across 55 achievements. Vice Got a Price!, the Platinum equivalent, is worth 0G, and A Long Road is the biggest at 100G.

What is the GTA 6 Platinum trophy called?

Vice Got a Price!, with the description “Congratulations! You’re a Leonida Legend.” It unlocks when you have every other trophy.

Are there missable trophies in GTA 6?

One risk: Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1, which counts toward Positive Mental Attitude and disappears after that chapter. Race Against Yago in Chapter 3 is also time-sensitive, so do it as soon as it appears.

How long does the GTA 6 Platinum take?

Around 200 hours. Most of it is the 100% checklist, which unlocks A Long Road.

Do cheats disable trophies in GTA 6?

They should. Cheats are expected to switch off trophies and saving for that session, so keep them off your trophy save.

Can you replay missions in GTA 6?

No. There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus at launch, which is why a manual save per chapter matters.
